# Pilot 01: Milo and the Little Lantern

An original bedtime story, read by a calm father. Runtime about 3 to 4 minutes.
Audience: young children and tired grown-ups. Set YouTube audience to "made for kids".

---

## 1. Story script (for Echo, the voice)

`[pause]` = about 2 seconds of quiet. `[long pause]` = about 4 seconds.

Settle in, little one. Pull the blanket up to your chin. That's it. `[pause]`

Tonight's story is about a small snail named Milo. `[pause]`

Milo lived at the bottom of a quiet garden, under a leaf as wide as a roof. And every evening, when the sun went to sleep, Milo lit his little lantern. `[pause]`

It was a tiny lantern, no bigger than a berry, and it glowed the colour of warm honey. `[pause]`

"Time to say goodnight," said Milo.

And off he went. Slowly, so slowly, because snails never hurry. `[pause]`

First, he came to the ladybird, asleep on her leaf. "Goodnight, ladybird," whispered Milo.

The ladybird gave a tiny yawn and tucked her wings in tight. "Goodnight, Milo," she said. `[pause]`

Slowly, so slowly, he went on. `[pause]`

Next, he came to the old brown toad, sitting by the garden gate. "Goodnight, toad," whispered Milo.

The toad gave one long, slow blink. "Goodnight, Milo. Mind how you go." `[pause]`

Slowly, so slowly, he went on. `[pause]`

Then he came to the tall sunflower, standing quietly in the dark. "Goodnight, sunflower," whispered Milo.

The sunflower bowed her big, heavy head. "Goodnight, Milo," she said. `[pause]`

Now Milo had said goodnight to everyone. And Milo was very, very tired. His eyes felt heavy. His shell felt warm. His little lantern glowed softer, and softer. `[pause]`

But Milo wondered, just for a moment, who says goodnight to the one who says goodnight to everyone? `[long pause]`

And then, high above the garden, the moon rose. Round and silver and kind. She looked down and smiled. `[pause]`

"Goodnight, Milo," whispered the moon.

And she laid a blanket of silver light across his little shell. `[pause]`

Milo tucked himself in. His lantern blinked once, twice, and went out. And he slept, all through the quiet, starry night. `[long pause]`

Now your eyes are getting heavy, too, little one. Your blanket is warm. The moon is watching over you. `[pause]`

Goodnight. `[long pause]`

*(about 310 words, about 3.5 minutes read slowly)*

## 3. Voice and sound notes (for Echo)

- **Voice:** calm adult male, warm, low pitch, like a father reading to his child.
- **Pace:** slow, about 100 to 115 words per minute. Slower still for the last paragraph.
- **Delivery:** soft, never loud. Smile in the voice. Do not act out the animals; just a gentle change of tone.
- **Pauses:** follow the `[pause]` marks. Keep the pauses even if they feel long.
- **Ending:** the final "Goodnight." is almost a whisper, followed by 4 seconds of quiet.
- **Background sound:**
  - Very soft night ambience (distant crickets or gentle breeze), about 20 to 25 dB under the voice.
  - A slow, warm, simple music pad underneath.
  - No sudden sounds, no drums, no loud changes. Fade out slowly in the last 10 seconds.
- **Licences:** use only music and sounds whose licence allows monetised YouTube use. Keep a note of where each one came from.
