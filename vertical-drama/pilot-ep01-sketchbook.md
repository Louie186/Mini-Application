# Vertical drama pilot: "The 7:40 Train" (Episode 1)

A 60-second vertical (9:16) love story. Original idea, no real people.
Purpose: a low-cost test of whether AI video can reach a quality we are happy with.

---

## Logline
Two Filipino workers in Doha ride the same 7:40 metro every morning and never speak.
When Mia picks up a sketchbook left on the seat, she finds page after page of drawings of herself, and a note that changes everything.

## Why it is built this way
AI video is weakest at lip-sync and keeping faces the same. This episode avoids those risks:
- Most lines are **inner voice (voice-over)**, not lips moving on screen.
- Many shots show **hands, objects, backs and side profiles**.
- Faces appear in only a few close shots, all made from the **same reference image**.

---

## Characters (use the same description in every prompt)

**MIA** (female, 27)
Filipina nurse. Shoulder-length dark brown hair in a low ponytail, warm brown eyes, small mole under left eye, light brown skin.
Wears navy blue nurse scrubs under a beige cardigan, a small silver cross necklace, white sneakers, a teal tote bag.

**PAOLO** (male, 29)
Filipino site engineer. Short black hair with a slight wave, clean-shaven, kind tired eyes, medium brown skin.
Wears a light grey work polo with a small orange safety logo, dark work trousers, a black backpack, a pencil behind his ear.

**The sketchbook**
Small, worn, dark green cover with a hand-drawn paper airplane on the front, an elastic band, pencil sketches inside.

**Setting**
A modern, clean metro carriage at 7:40 in the morning, soft golden sunrise through the windows, a desert city skyline passing by.

## Video prompts (for Higgsfield or a similar image-to-video tool)

How to use: first make **one still image** for each character from their description (the "reference"), then make each shot from that image, so faces stay the same. Add this line to every prompt:

> vertical 9:16, cinematic, soft golden morning light, shallow depth of field, realistic, natural subtle movement, no text

1. Wide establishing shot of a modern desert city at sunrise, a sleek metro train arriving at an elevated station, warm golden light, slow camera push in.
2. Medium shot inside a clean modern metro carriage, MIA (see reference) sitting by the window wearing earphones, watching the city pass, gentle sway of the train.
3. Close-up of a man's hand drawing with a pencil in a dark green sketchbook on his lap, PAOLO's grey work sleeve visible, soft focus background of the carriage.
4. Close-up of MIA glancing to the side, then looking away with a small shy smile, sunlight on her face.
5. Medium shot of PAOLO looking up from his sketchbook toward the window seat, a hopeful expression, then looking down again.
6. Train stopping at a platform, PAOLO standing quickly and walking out through the doors, seen from behind, holding a phone to his ear.
7. Close-up of a worn dark green sketchbook with a paper airplane drawing on the cover, lying alone on an empty metro seat, morning light.
8. MIA picking up the sketchbook and looking toward the closing doors, the platform moving away outside.
9. Overhead close-up of a woman's hands opening a sketchbook, pencil drawings of a metro train and city skyline on the pages.
10. Close-up of a sketchbook page with a detailed pencil portrait of a young woman by a train window wearing earphones; her hand touches the page.
11. Hands slowly turning sketchbook pages showing pencil drawings of the same woman laughing, reading, sleeping against a window, small handwritten dates.
12. Close-up of MIA's face, surprised and touched, eyes glistening, soft smile, golden light.
13. Close-up of the last sketchbook page: a pencil drawing of two coffee cups and a handwritten note (add the note text in editing, not in the AI video).
14. Close-up of a phone in a woman's hand lighting up with a message notification (add the message text in editing).
15. MIA looking from her phone to the sketchbook, conflicted, slow push in, fade to black.

## Voices and sound
- MIA's inner voice: soft, warm, young woman. PAOLO: one short line, friendly, slightly rushed.
- Options: the owner's own voice cannot be used for Mia, so use a paid voice tool, or ask a friend (with permission).
- Music: soft piano, free library with a licence that allows monetised use.
- On-screen text (time, note, message) is added in editing (CapCut), not generated by the AI video tool.

---

## Safe test plan (before paying for anything big)
1. Use free trial credits only. Make the two character reference images first.
2. Test the 3 hardest shots first: #2 (Mia in the train), #10 (the portrait page), #12 (Mia's face).
3. If those 3 pass the checklist below, make the rest. If not, stop. Nothing lost but time.
4. Budget cap: 70 QAR for the whole pilot. Expect 2 to 3 tries per shot.

## Quality checklist (all must be "yes" to continue the series)
- [ ] Mia looks like the same person in every shot.
- [ ] Movement looks natural (no melting hands or faces).
- [ ] The story is clear without sound.
- [ ] The ending makes you want to see Episode 2.
- [ ] It looks good on a phone screen.
