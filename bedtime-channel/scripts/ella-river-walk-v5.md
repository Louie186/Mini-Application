# Little Ella's River Walk (v5 reading script)

Read it like you are telling it to your own child at your side. Slow, warm, smiling.
About 35 seconds. `( … )` = how to say it. `/` = short pause. `//` = longer pause.

---

**Picture 1: walking with Mama and Papa**

*(warm, proud, gentle smile)*
Once upon a time, little Ella the elephant went for a walk with Mama and Papa. //

*(playful, each stomp a little bigger, like little feet hitting the ground)*
Stomp… / stomp… / STOMP! //

**Picture 2: they see the river**

*(curious, a little secret in your voice, leaning in)*
Then Ella saw something sparkly. /

*(surprised and excited, eyes wide)*
The river! //

**Picture 3: drinking and splashing**

*(playful, enjoying the sound)*
Slurp! / Splash! /

*(happy, satisfied sigh, like after a cold drink on a hot day)*
Ahh… the water was so cool and yummy. //

**Picture 4: walking on**

*(warm and content)*
Then, with happy tummies, off they went again… /

*(cheerful)*
on their happy, happy journey. /

*(joyful, a small cheer, not a shout)*
Hooray! //

---

## Tips for reading it yourself
- Smile while you read. It can be heard in the voice.
- Slow down more than feels normal, especially before "The river!" and "Ahh…".
- Let the voice go up for surprise ("The river!") and down for comfort ("Ahh…").
- Record on your phone in a quiet room, about a hand's length from your mouth.
- One full take is fine; small mistakes make it feel real.
