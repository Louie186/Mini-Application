Hover tips
Transmittal log
Reminders
Ctrl+K in any tab searches everything (tasks, drawings, transmittals, people, files).
Weekly report
Saved in exports\Weekly reports (a page + text). The bell and Home tell you when it's ready – open it or copy it into an email. If the Toolbox isn't open that day, it's made the next time it opens that week.
Health check
Looks over your data, folders and links and tells you what needs attention – nothing is changed unless you click a fix.
Update the Toolbox (safely)
Got a new version? Extract the zip first, then pick its folder (the one with LewsToolbox.hta). The program you have now is backed up first (Backups\Program …); your data is never touched. Something wrong? Go back puts the old version back.
Your name
Shown as "Issued by" on task sheets you send.
Data folder
Where your lists are saved. Put it in OneDrive to use the same data on every PC.
Backups
Every day, the first time you open the Toolbox, your data is copied to Backups\<date> in the data folder (the last 14 days are kept). Your task folders aren't touched – they're already your own files.
Launcher list
Use the Toolbox's own list, or share the same list with your QuickLaunch.hta (pick the folder that has QuickLaunch.hta in it).
Link to Lew's Tracker (optional)
Pick the web app's data folder (the one with Delegation.json) to see those tasks in Delegate. The Toolbox only reads it.
Taskbar
Puts a "Lew's Toolbox" shortcut on your Desktop: right-click it > Pin to taskbar (Windows 11: Show more options first).
